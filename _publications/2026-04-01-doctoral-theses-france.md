A preprint presenting a linked dataset of French doctoral theses (1985–2025) that combines information on PhDs, academic networks, and institutional affiliations, based on the Theses.fr dataset. The dataset supports research on gender dynamics in doctoral juries, co-supervision networks, and disciplinary clustering in French academia.

## Bio

Political Scientist with more than 7 years of experience specializing in Democratization Studies, Migration Studies, and Middle Eastern Studies with a methodological focus on quantitative and qualitative data analysis.  
Fluent in German, English, French, and Kurdish, with extensive experience working in diverse cultural settings, particularly in the Middle East.  
Expertise spanning academia and freelance political consulting, focusing on utilizing data to inform strategic decision-making.  
Experienced public speaker and educator with strong communication and analytical skills.

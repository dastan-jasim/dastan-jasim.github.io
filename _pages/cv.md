## Bio

Political scientist and computational social scientist with expertise in democratization studies, political behavior and culture, Middle East and Kurdish studies, and computational text analysis. Research spans field-based mixed-methods work, large-scale survey data, and NLP/LLM applications to political and social questions. Fluent in German, English, French, and Kurdish; experienced researcher, public speaker, and policy advisor.
